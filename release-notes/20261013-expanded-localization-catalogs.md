The Core catalogs now contain 2,230 non-empty entries in Spanish, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Hindi, German, Japanese, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Arabic, Korean, Vietnamese, and Indonesian. Sixty-five current English keys are absent from each catalog and continue to fall back to English. The catalogs combine existing translations, offline machine translation, and unchanged product or technical terms; fluent-speaker review remains open.
